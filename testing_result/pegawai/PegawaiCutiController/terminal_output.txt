## 2. Masalah Utama

- Controller terlalu mengetahui detail pembuatan dan dependensi objek.
- Ketergantungan sulit diganti atau diuji secara terisolasi.
- Terdapat duplikasi kecil yang berpotensi berkembang menjadi inkonsistensi.
- Kompleksitas siklomatik sebesar 6 menunjukkan adanya beberapa percabangan yang perlu dikendalikan.
- Volume Halstead relatif tinggi terhadap ukuran kode, yang mengindikasikan logika cukup padat atau menggunakan banyak operator/operand.

## 3. Rekomendasi Architectural Refactoring

1. **Terapkan Dependency Injection**
   - Pindahkan pembuatan objek dari Controller ke dependency container, composition root, atau konfigurasi aplikasi.
   - Inject dependency melalui konstruktor atau mekanisme framework yang sesuai.

2. **Pisahkan tanggung jawab Controller dan Business Logic**
   - Controller sebaiknya hanya menangani input, validasi dasar, pemanggilan service, dan pembentukan response.
   - Pindahkan proses bisnis ke service/use-case layer.

3. **Gunakan abstraksi interface untuk dependency utama**
   - Definisikan kontrak untuk komponen yang mungkin berubah, seperti repository, service eksternal, atau provider.
   - Hal ini meningkatkan testability dan reusability.

4. **Eliminasi duplikasi**
   - Ekstrak baris yang berulang ke helper, shared service, atau fungsi utilitas yang memiliki tanggung jawab jelas.
   - Hindari membuat abstraksi berlebihan untuk duplikasi yang sangat kecil dan tidak stabil.

5. **Sederhanakan percabangan**
   - Pisahkan kondisi kompleks ke metode bernama jelas atau gunakan strategy/policy pattern jika variasi perilaku semakin bertambah.
   - Kurangi nested conditional agar alur utama lebih mudah dibaca.

6. **Evaluasi kembali struktur modul**
   - Pastikan dependency mengarah dari Controller ke service/use-case, lalu ke repository atau adapter.
   - Hindari Controller bergantung langsung pada implementasi konkret.

## 4. Prioritas Rekomendasi

### Prioritas Tinggi
1. Terapkan **Dependency Injection** untuk menghilangkan direct object instantiation di Controller.
2. Pisahkan business logic ke **service/use-case layer**.
3. Hilangkan duplikasi kode yang teridentifikasi.

### Prioritas Menengah
4. Gunakan interface dan adapter untuk dependency yang dapat berubah.
5. Refactor percabangan untuk menurunkan kompleksitas dan meningkatkan keterbacaan.

### Prioritas Rendah
6. Lakukan evaluasi struktur modul dan abstraksi secara berkala.
7. Tambahkan pengujian unit pada service dan dependency yang telah dipisahkan.

## Overall Evaluation

The refactoring is **partially successful and generally beneficial**. The code became easier to maintain through reduced size, lower complexity, and removal of the direct object-instantiation issue. However, maintainability remains in the **Medium** category, and reusability slightly declined due to increased external dependencies.

## Improvements

- Maintainability improved from **67.94 to 73.35**, indicating better overall code structure.
- Lines of code and Halstead volume decreased, suggesting reduced implementation complexity.
- Cyclomatic complexity decreased from **6 to 3**, making the control flow easier to understand, test, and modify.
- Direct object instantiation in the controller was eliminated, indicating better separation of responsibilities through a request class and service class.
- The controller appears more focused, with business logic likely moved into `PegawaiCutiService`.
- No long-method, large-class, or error-handling issues were identified.

## Remaining Issues

- Maintainability is still categorized as **Medium**, so further simplification and structural improvement may be possible.
- Coupling increased from **6 to 8 dependencies**. Although introducing a service and form request can improve architecture, the controller now depends on more components and abstractions. These dependencies should remain purposeful and well managed.
- Reusability declined slightly from **0.7073 to 0.6934**, likely due to the increased dependency count.
- Duplicated code increased from two repeated lines to three. This should be reviewed, especially if the duplication occurs across the controller, service, or request classes.
- No explicit error-handling issues were detected, but the available metrics do not confirm whether service failures, validation failures, and authentication problems are handled consistently.

## Recommendation

Accept the refactoring as a **positive improvement**, particularly because complexity and maintainability improved and controller responsibilities were better separated. Before considering the work complete:

- Remove or centralize the remaining duplicated code.
- Review the additional dependencies and ensure they follow dependency-inversion principles.
- Add or strengthen automated tests for the service, request validation, authorization, and failure paths.
- Reassess maintainability after these changes to determine whether it can move beyond the Medium category.
